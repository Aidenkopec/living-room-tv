# Screensaver photos ("Beautiful places")

**Status: approved (2026-10-19).** Phase 3 downloads exactly these files and nothing else (see "Downloading the photos safely" in the design doc), with `scripts/fetch-screensaver-photos.sh`.

To look at them, open `docs/screensaver-preview.html` in a browser. It shows every photo numbered, grouped by day, dusk and night, cropped to the TV's shape, with an optional mock of the clock and caption on top. The pictures on that page come straight from Wikimedia's own small previews; no image files are saved in this folder.

## How they were picked (my call, inside your rules)

- **Your rules:** about 60 photos of the most beautiful places in the world, about 5 from Banff, no more than about 4 per place, at least 10 night photos, landscape, at least 1920 pixels wide, no people in focus, no text or watermarks, fairly calm bottom corners (the clock sits bottom-left and the caption bottom-right), and only public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA licences.
- **My call:** most come from Wikimedia Commons' "Featured pictures" (the best-rated photos on the site), the rest from its "Quality images". Each one was looked at with the clock and caption laid over it.
- **Checked for every photo:** the Commons page exists, the licence is one of the four allowed, the file is a JPEG, and it's at least 1920 pixels wide. The exact file size and fingerprint (SHA-1) are recorded so the download step can check it got the right file.

## At a glance

- **61 photos:** 32 day, 14 dusk, 15 night.
- **Banff:** 5 (Moraine Lake 2, Lake Louise 2, Peyto Lake 1).
- **Most from one place:** 2. Moraine Lake, Lake Louise, the Matterhorn, the Dolomites, La Digue and Oia have 2 each; everywhere else has 1.
- **Licences:** 47 CC BY-SA, 8 CC BY, 5 CC0, 1 public domain. CC BY and CC BY-SA need the photographer credited; the app's credits list uses the "Credit line" below. CC BY-SA also means that if you ever share the resized copies, they carry the same licence.
- **Download size:** the original files add up to about 893 MB, because many are huge (up to 16,384 pixels wide). The resized TV copies will still come to the planned 30–40 MB. **Decided (2026-10-19):** download the originals as listed, so each one is checked against its fingerprint (SHA-1) below. (The other choice was Wikimedia's ready-made 3840-pixel-wide copies: a smaller download, but with no fingerprint to check.) The resized copies are kept out of git; the script can make them again.
- **Go slowly when downloading (my call, learned while checking this list):** Wikimedia's file server started refusing requests ("too many requests") after about ten quick checks in a row. The Phase 3 download should fetch one file every few seconds and wait and retry when refused, rather than treat a refusal as a bad file.

- **Skógafoss Waterfall, Iceland, 20240720 1318 2975.jpg:** a person walking towards the falls.
- **Geirangerfjord close-up of a cruise ship**, **Reine houses (Lofoten)**, **Lago di Braies 3.jpg:** busy bottoms (ship, houses, rows of boats) right where the clock and caption go.
- **John Ford's Point Monument Valley:** a rider on a horse in focus, and only 2000 pixels wide.
- **Wadi Rum BW 16.JPG:** jeeps in the middle.
- **Salar de Uyuni "Watching sunset":** a person's silhouette in the middle.
- **Aurora Australis over the Tasman Sea** and **"Human looking at the stars during Perseids"**: a person in the middle of each.
- **Shibuya Crossing** and **Night in Shinjuku:** crowds and shop signs (text).
- **Maldives floatplane:** writing on the plane.
- **Ha Long Bay:** no photo found without cruise boats or fishing boats crowding it.
- **Bora Bora:** no suitable photo turned up (searches found unrelated files).

- **Yunnan rice terraces:** a very busy pattern right behind the clock.
- **Széchenyi Chain Bridge, Budapest (CC0):** beautiful, but kept to one Budapest photo (the Parliament). Easy swap.

- **Grand Canyon Hopi Point with rainbow** and **Everest, Himalayas.jpg (daytime):** good, but the evening Grand Canyon and sunset Everest were chosen instead.
- **Cappadocia balloon close-ups:** the balloon fills the frame; the wider balloon-start photo was chosen.
